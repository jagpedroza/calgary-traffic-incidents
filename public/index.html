About the data

Source: Traffic Incidents — Open Calgary, published by The City of Calgary. Updated continuously from the Advanced Traveler Information System (ATIS), December 2016 to present.

According to the City, this is an unofficial archive of incidents affecting traffic flow (signal issues, hazardous road conditions, stalled vehicles and unverified collisions) based on traffic-camera imagery. It is not an official collision record and may have gaps. The incident type shown here is derived automatically from the description text.
